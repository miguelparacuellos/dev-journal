# Dev Journal

A personal journal for recalling daily work and preparing follow-up conversations.

## Language

**Daily log**:
The collection of entries describing what happened during a workday.

**Daily**:
A brief check-in meeting to share recent work and today's plan.

**One to one (O2O)**:
A monthly meeting with the tech lead to discuss feedback, proposals, and follow-up topics.
_Avoid_: OtoO, 121

**Task**:
An action the user wants to complete in the future.
_Avoid_: Todo, to-do

**Resource**:
Material the user wants to consult, such as a video, reading, or training course.

**Entry**:
A note about an outcome, progress, or event during the day; it does not require time tracking.

**Today's plan**:
The selection of actions intended for the current day, distinct from completed work.

**Blocker**:
A dated note about an obstacle requiring help or resolution, separate from
completed progress and intended actions. It belongs to the day on which the
obstacle was recorded.

**Daily proposal**:
A draft of what to share in the daily, organized into recent work, today's plan, and blockers.

**O2O topic**:
An item to discuss in a one to one that remains open until it has been addressed.

**O2O agreement**:
A conclusion or commitment recorded during a one to one.

**Addressed topic**:
An O2O topic discussed in a specific one to one. It is no longer open and stays
recorded with that meeting.

**Follow-up task**:
A task created during a one to one and associated with that meeting. It is
planned and completed like any other task.
