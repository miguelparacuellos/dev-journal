# 04: Collect and review O2O topics

**What to build:** Capture O2O topics throughout the month and review the persistent collection of open topics before the next meeting.

**Blocked by:** 01: Capture and browse the daily log.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Users can add free-text O2O topics without starting a meeting.
- [ ] The O2O view clearly presents open topics for meeting preparation and has a helpful empty state.
- [ ] Open topics remain available across days and application restarts.
- [ ] Topic capture and review are usable by keyboard with visible actions and English UI text.
- [ ] Behavior tests through the public application interface verify topic capture, review, and persistence independently of meeting recording.
